## Project 01 — Customer Churn Analysis

**End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python | SQL | PostgreSQL | Power BI**

An end-to-end customer churn analytics project focused on identifying churn-associated customer segments, analyzing customer risk, quantifying recurring revenue exposure, and identifying potential retention opportunities.

### Project Workflow

```text
Business Problem
      ↓
Data Validation
      ↓
Data Cleaning
      ↓
Python EDA
      ↓
KPI Analysis
      ↓
SQL / PostgreSQL Analysis
      ↓
Risk Segmentation
      ↓
Revenue Analysis
      ↓
Power BI Dashboard
      ↓
Business Insights
      ↓
Retention Recommendations
```

### Key Results

- **7,032 customers** analyzed after data cleaning
- **26.58%** overall observed churn rate
- **42.71%** observed churn among month-to-month customers
- **45.29%** observed churn among Electronic Check customers
- **47.68%** observed churn among customers with 0–12 months tenure
- **74.45%** observed churn at Risk Score 6
- **910** high-value churned customers identified
- **139.13K** in monthly charges associated with churned customers
- **1,182** active customers in high-risk segments
- **89.89K** in associated monthly revenue among high-risk active customers

## Project 03 — Sales & Profitability Analysis

**End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python | SQL | PostgreSQL | Power BI**

A sales and profitability analytics project focused on understanding revenue, profit, profit margins, discount patterns, customer profitability, product performance, regional performance, shipping performance, and monthly trends.

### Project Workflow

```text

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
Regional Analysis
      ↓
PostgreSQL
      ↓
SQL Business Analysis
      ↓
Power BI Dashboard
      ↓
Business Insights
```

### Current Results

- **10,194** sales records analyzed
- **5,111** unique orders
- **804** customers
- **1,862** products
- **$2.33M** total sales
- **$292.30K** total profit
- **12.56%** overall profit margin
- **$455.20** average order value
- **3.96 days** average shipping time
- **159** loss-making customers identified
- **3** loss-making sub-categories identified
- **2** loss-making months identified in the analyzed period

### Key Profitability Analysis

The project evaluates the relationship between discounts and profitability.

Observed profit margins by discount band:

```text
No Discount     → 29.56%
Low Discount    → 11.86%
Medium Discount → -15.29%
High Discount   → -77.37%
```

Loss-making sub-categories identified include:

```text
Tables
Bookcases
Supplies
```

The project also analyzes:

- Customer profitability
- Product profitability
- High-sales / low-profit products
- Regional profitability
- Shipping performance
- Monthly sales and profit
- Category loss rates
